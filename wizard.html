How to play Wizard

60 cards: four colours numbered 1 to 13, plus 4 Wizards and 4 Jesters. Round 1 deals one card to everyone, round 2 two cards, and so on until the deck runs out (20 rounds with 3 players, 15 with 4, 12 with 5, 10 with 6).

  • Trump: after dealing, the next card is turned over and its colour is trump. A Jester means no trump this round; a Wizard lets the dealer pick. In the last round there is no trump.
  • Bidding: starting left of the dealer, everyone says how many tricks they will take.
  • Playing: follow the colour that was led if you can. A Wizard or a Jester can always be played instead.
  • Who takes the trick: the first Wizard played; otherwise the highest trump; otherwise the highest card in the colour led. Jesters never win (unless everyone played one, then the first does).
  • Scoring: exactly right gets 20 points plus 10 per trick. Off by any amount loses 10 points per trick you were off.

Wizard here is played for points, not chips. Win a game to earn the Archmage badge.
