How to play

Bet on which hand ends closer to 9: Player or Banker, or on a Tie. Cards count at face value, aces are 1 and tens and pictures are 0. Only the last digit of the total counts, so 7 + 8 = 5.

Both hands get two cards. With 8 or 9 (a "natural") nobody draws. Otherwise the Player draws a third card on 0 to 5, and the Banker draws by the standard table rules. The dealer handles all of it.

  • Player pays 1 : 1.
  • Banker pays 0.95 : 1 (a 5% commission, because the Banker wins a little more often).
  • Tie pays 8 : 1, and Player and Banker bets are returned on a tie.
  • Player Pair and Banker Pair pay 11 : 1 when that hand's first two cards are the same rank.

Everyone at the table plays the same hand, dealt from an 8-deck shoe by the casino's server. Player and Banker take $1 to $1,000; Tie and pairs up to $200 (VIPs can bet five times as much).
