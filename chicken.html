How to play

Get the chicken across the road. Pick your bet and difficulty and press Play. Every press of Go hops the chicken into the next lane. Make it and the multiplier on that manhole is yours to keep, and a barrier goes up behind you. Get hit by a car and the bet is gone.

Cash out whenever you like. Reach the far side and you cash out automatically at the top multiplier.

  • Easy: 24 lanes. The first lane is a 4% risk, and it rises to about 21% by the last. Up to 24×.
  • Medium: 22 lanes, 12% rising to 46%. Up to 2,200×.
  • Hard: 20 lanes, 20% rising to 61%. Up to 50,000×.
  • Hardcore: 15 lanes, 40% rising to 82%. Up to 3,000,000×.

The further you go, the busier the traffic. The casino's server decides each lane the moment you hop into it, so not even this page knows what's coming. Every multiplier pays back 97% of the risk to reach it. A single crossing pays at most $250,000.
