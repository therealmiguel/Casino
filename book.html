Tomb of Amun-Ra

5 reels, 3 rows and 10 lines. Your bet is spread over all 10 lines. Wins pay left to right on a line, from the first reel.

Pays (times the line bet)

The Book

The Book stands in for every symbol, and it is also the scatter: 3, 4 or 5 anywhere pay 2×, 20× or 200× your whole bet and open the tomb for 10 free spins.

Free spins

Before they start, the book flips open on one symbol. During the free spins, whenever that symbol lands on enough reels (2 for the picture symbols, 3 for the letters), it expands to fill each of those reels and pays on all 10 lines, even if the reels aren't next to each other. 3 more books give 10 more spins.

Gamble

After a win in the normal game you can gamble it: guess whether the next card is red or black. Right doubles the win, wrong loses it. Up to 5 guesses in a row, or collect anytime.

The machine pays back about 96% over time, wins are capped at 5,000× the bet, and every spin feeds the Mega Jackpot. The casino's server decides every spin and every card.
