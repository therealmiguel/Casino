How to play

Pick your bet, the risk and how many rows of pins the board has, then drop a ball. It bounces left or right off every pin and lands in a bucket at the bottom. The bucket's number is what your bet is multiplied by.

The middle buckets are the most likely and pay the least. The outside ones are rare and pay the most. More rows and higher risk mean bigger edges and smaller middles. You can have lots of balls in the air at once.

The casino's server decides every bounce the moment you drop, so the ball's path is set before it falls. Over a very long time each board pays back between 97.5% and 98.5% of what is bet.
