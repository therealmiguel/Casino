How to play

Choose your bet and how many mines hide under the 25 tiles, then press Start. Every tile you open that hides a gem raises your multiplier. Cash out whenever you like to take your bet times the multiplier. Open a mine and the bet is lost.

More mines means every gem is worth much more. With 24 mines a single gem pays 24.25×. The "Next tile" chip shows what the next gem would pay.

The casino's server hides the mines when you press Start and doesn't tell your browser where they are until the game ends. Multipliers are set so the game pays back 97% over time. A single game pays at most $250,000.
