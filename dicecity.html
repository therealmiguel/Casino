How to play

Two dice are rolled and the taxi drives to the lot with their total, from 2 to 12. Everyone online plays the same roll.

  • Low wins on 2 to 6, High on 8 to 12, and Seven on 7. They pay your bet times the gold multiplier on the lot the taxi stops at, so a Low bet pays more when a 2 comes up than when a 6 does.
  • Exact total bets win only on that number and pay the blue multiplier shown on the button.
  • You can bet on several spots at once. A Low bet and a bet on 4 both win when a 4 is rolled, each with its own multiplier.

Building time. When bets close, 1 to 5 skyscrapers go up on random lots before the roll. Each one multiplies that lot's gold and blue multipliers (by different amounts), and towers can stack. A single bet can pay up to 300×.

The casino's server builds and rolls; this page only shows it. Over time Low, Seven and High pay back about 97% and exact totals about 96%. Low, Seven and High take $1 to $1,000, exact totals up to $200 (VIPs can bet five times as much).
